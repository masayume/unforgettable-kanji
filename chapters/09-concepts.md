Imagine making a solemn pact with a friend. You tie a piece of **thread (糸)** around each other's fingers as a reminder, and use a small **ladle (勺)** to carefully measure out an exact, shared portion.
\hl{Using a thread to bind an agreement and carefully measuring out your words creates a firm promise, a set agreement, or an exact approximate limit.}

**On\'yomi: : ヤク (YAKU)**

- 約束 (やくそく / yakusoku) – promise, appointment. (束: bundle, manage, control)
- 約 (やく / yaku) – approximately, about (e.g., 約10人 / yaku ju-nin – about 10 people).
- 予約 (よやく / yoyaku) – reservation, booking.
