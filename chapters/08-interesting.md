**Sabi 寂 (さび, ジャク, セキ) - Loneliness. Tranquility. - 1599 of 2500**

\begin{tabular}{|m{6cm}|m{0.8cm}|m{1.4cm}|m{0.8cm}|m{6cm}|}
\hline
Upper Radical & & Kanji & & Lower Radical \\
\hline
(ukanmuri) - The "roof" radical, depicting the roof of a house. & \Huge 宀 & \scalebox{2}{\Huge 寂} & \Huge 叔 & "uncle" (specifically a younger uncle, as seen in 叔父 oji). \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

Imagine a large, **empty house with an old roof (宀)**. Inside, your **quiet uncle (叔)** sits all by himself in the dark without any visitors. \hl{Sitting alone under a roof with just your quiet uncle leaves the entire home feeling empty, quiet, and lonely.}

**On\'yomi: ジャク (JAKU), セキ (SEKI)**

- 静寂 (せいじゃく / seijaku) – silence, quietude, stillness.
- 寂滅 (じゃくめつ / jakumetsu) – absolute peace, nirvana (Buddhist term).
- 寂寞 (せきばく / sekibaku) – loneliness, desolation.

**Kun\'yomi: さび-しい (sabi.shii), さび-れる (sabi.reru)**

- 侘び寂び (わびさび) - **wabi-sabi**; aesthetic sense in Japanese art centered on the acceptance of transience and imperfection.
- 寂しい (さびしい / sabishii) – lonely, lonesome.
- 寂れる (さびれる / sabireru) – to become desolate, to decline (e.g., a deserted town).

**Kake 駆 (かけ.る、 か.る) - drive, run, gallop, advance. - 1033 of 2500**

\begin{tabular}{|m{6cm}|m{0.8cm}|m{1.4cm}|m{0.8cm}|m{6cm}|}
\hline
Left Radical & & Kanji & & Right Radical \\
\hline
(umahen) - The "horse" radical. & \Huge 馬 & \scalebox{2}{\Huge 駆} & \Huge 区 & (ku) This kanji means "ward," "district," or "designated area". \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

Imagine a royal messenger riding a **powerful horse (馬)** at top speed, charging from one **city district (区)** to the next to deliver a vital message. \hl{A horse speeding through every district is the ultimate image of galloping and driving forward.}

**On\'yomi: ク (KU)**

- 駆使 (くし / kushi) – making full use of, mastering/driving a skill.
- 駆除 (くじょ / kujo) – extermination, pest control (literally "driving out and removing").
- 先駆者 (せんくしゃ / senkusha) – pioneer, forerunner (literally "one who runs ahead").

**Kun\'yomi: さび-しい (ja.keru), か-ける (ka.keru)**

- 駆ける (かける / kakeru) – to run, to gallop.
- 駆け足 (かけあし / kakeashi) – running pace, fast step.
- 駆 (駆け引き / **kakehiki**) - bargaining, strategy, manouvering.

**Kyou 興 (コウ、 キョウ) - entertain, revive, retrieve, interest, pleasure - 734 of 2500**

\begin{tabular}{|m{6cm}|m{0.8cm}|m{1.4cm}|m{0.8cm}|m{6cm}|}
\hline
Center Radical & & Kanji & & Surrpunding Radical \\
\hline
(usu) — This radical means "mortar" & \Huge 臼 & \scalebox{2}{\Huge 興} & \Huge 同 & Multiple pairs of hands grasping and lifting something up together. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}

When everyone works together with the **same goal** to lift something up, the town prospers and it creates a great sense of **interest and excitement**. \hl{A village comes together with the **same (同) goal** in mind. Everyone gathers around and uses all of their **hands (the four corner pieces)** to lift up a massive, **heavy mortar (臼)** to pound mochi for a festival.}

**On\'yomi: キョウ (KYŌ), コウ (KŌ)**

- 興味 (きょうみ / kyōmi) – interest, curiosity.
- 興奮 (こうふん / kōfun) – excitement, stimulation.
- 復興 (ふっこう / fukkō) – revival, reconstruction, recovery.

**Kun\'yomi: おこ-る (oko.ru), おこ-す (oko.su)**

- 興る (おこる / okoru) – to rise, to flourish, to prosper (intransitive).
- 興す (おこす / okosu) – to revive, to launch, to establish (transitive).
