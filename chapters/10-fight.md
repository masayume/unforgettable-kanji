Two neighboring clans who have a disagreement over who owns a **single, simple (単) rice field (田)**. What starts as a simple argument quickly escalates until both sides grab their **spears (戈)** and charge into the field.
\hl{Crossing your spears over a simple plot of land is how a full-blown war and battle breaks out.}

**On\'yomi: : セン (SEN)** 

- 戦争 (せんそう / sensō) – war; 争 (contend, dispute, argue).
- 作戦 (さくせん / sakusen) – tactics, strategy, military operation.
- 挑戦 (ちょうせん / chōsen) – challenge, defiance (e.g., trying something difficult).

**Kun\'yomi: たたか-う (tataka.u), いくさ (ikusa)**

- 戦う (たたかう / tatakau) – to fight, to battle, to struggle against.
- 戦 (いくさ / ikusa) – war, battle (An older, historical word for war, often heard in samurai dramas).
